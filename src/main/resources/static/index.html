How it works

From the depot to your pocket

Step 01 · Broadcast

The bus goes on air

Each driver's device streams GPS coordinates and measured speed over a persistent WebSocket the moment a shift starts.

Step 02 · Fan-out

The server routes it, route-scoped

Positions are matched to routes and fanned out only to riders watching that line — keeping the live feed lean.

Step 03 · Arrive

You get an honest ETA

Your map interpolates the bus along the line and computes minutes-to-arrival from real speed, not a fixed schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the bus tracking?
Our GPS tracking system provides real-time location updates with an accuracy of within 50 meters. Updates are refreshed every 30 seconds.
Is there a mobile app available?
Yes! Our mobile app is available for both Android and iOS devices. You can download it from Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
What should I do if a bus doesn't arrive?
If a bus is delayed or doesn't arrive, please contact our customer support immediately. We'll help track the bus and provide alternative options.
